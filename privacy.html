EAT SLOW QUEST PLAY ▶

PRIVACY

The short version: the game keeps your progress on your own device. The only personal data I receive is what you choose to send through the feedback form.

WHO IS RESPONSIBLE

Eat Slow Quest is a free hobby project by Patryk Mazurkiewicz, who is the controller of your personal data under the GDPR. Contact: pmaz133@gmail.com.

THE GAME

Your hero, level, gear and meal log are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never sent to me or anyone else. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no ads.

To delete this data, use Start over in My Meals, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. Save codes you copy contain the same data and stay wherever you paste them.

THE FEEDBACK FORM

If you send feedback, the form collects your email address, your rating (optional) and your message.

  • Why: to read your feedback and reply to you. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by sending the form. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing me.
  • Where it goes: the form is delivered by FormSubmit, a form-forwarding service, which emails it to my Gmail inbox (Google). Both services may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States.
  • How long: I keep messages only as long as needed to handle your feedback, and delete them sooner if you ask.
  • I never sell your data, add you to a mailing list, or use it for anything else.

HOSTING

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you visit, and may log it for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Fonts are served from this site itself, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts or other third parties while you play. The research link on the home page opens the publisher's own site.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask me to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, or for a copy of it. Email pmaz133@gmail.com and I'll reply within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).
