Why eat slowly?

Feeling full takes around twenty minutes to register. Eat faster than that and you finish before your body says "enough".

Research backs this up: people who eat more slowly tend to eat less, and don't feel hungrier afterwards.

Robinson et al., American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 2014
